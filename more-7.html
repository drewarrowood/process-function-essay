The sum, in ordinary arithmetic

Sum over the eight internal strings. Each term is a product of three overlaps, one per desk. On the diagonal, unit rows make each admitted string contribute 1. A process has one such string, so the diagonal is 1. The copy rule has eight, so the diagonal is 8. Off the diagonal, the witness party contributes the inner product of two different columns of an orthonormal row, which is 0. A product with a 0 is 0. That is the cancellation. It needs the rows. It needs the ban on two finishes, so that a differing pair is not both admitted.

Diagonal of a process: 1. Diagonal of the copy rule: 8. Off-diagonal with a witness: 0.

What Lugano is not asked to do

Lugano is a particular process function on three bits. This page does not ask for its table. It asks for the count. The copy rule is not Lugano. The copy rule is the control, the map with eight finishes. A process function is whatever map has one finish for every local rule. The lift is stated for that class, not for one named table.
