What a party is

A party is a label on a bit, not an event. Three parties means a string of length 3. The process is a function of the whole string. The intervention at a party sees only that party's received bit. Oreshkov, Costa and Brukner make the same separation for channels. The count is the extra. A curve joins events. A string does not. That is why a metric is not a premise of the formula.

What the 2019 paper left open

Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf and Zych have the open shuffle and leave the quantum step open. The copy rule has that inverse and does not have the closed unitary. Recovery of the spare page still works on the days the loop is locked. The eight consistent slips are a different fact. They do not cancel. Unique finish is what supplies the witness that kills the extra slips. Without that premise, the closed map keeps every slip.

The sentence

Every process function lifts, by the source-and-sink shuffle, to a source-to-sink unitary, once the local rows are orthonormal.

The open shuffle inverts for every rule, because XOR twice is the bit. The closed copy rule has eight finishes on three parties: 000 through 111. The witness exists only because two finishes are forbidden. If two strings differ and the process returns the same bit at that party, an orthonormal row makes that party a zero. Drop the rows and the argument stops. No metric is used. The temporal file records the inverse and the eight. It does not record the zero.
