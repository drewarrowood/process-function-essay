Witness at the second desk

Two strings differ only at the second desk. Call them 000 and 010. The process returns the same bit at that desk. The second factor is the inner product of two different columns of an orthonormal row. That factor is 0. A zero at the second person is enough. The first desk and the third desk can agree. The term is still 0.

Differ only at B, same received bit at B, orthonormal row: factor at B is 0, so the term is 0.

On two parties the copy rule has diagonal 4. On three, 8. The open inverse holds in both cases. The witness zero holds only when two finishes are forbidden and the rows are orthonormal. This page is the explanation. It is not a new theorem. No metric is constructed.
