  17. Mateus Araújo, Adrien Feix, Miguel Navascués, and Časlav Brukner, “A Purification Postulate for Quantum Mechanics with Indefinite Causal Order”, Quantum 1 (2017).
  18. Augustin Vanrietvelde, Nick Ormrod, Hlér Kristjánsson, and Jonathan Barrett, routed-circuit reconstruction of the Lugano unitary extension. Existence proof for one function, not the general argument.
  19. Daher Ahmed and Ravi Kunjwal, arXiv:2610.00579 (2026). Purification of process functions under mutual exclusivity and unambiguity.

  22. A. Carlini, V. P. Frolov, M. B. Mensky, I. D. Novikov, and S. N. Solodukhin, “Time Machines: The Principle of Self-Consistency as a Consequence of the Principle of Minimal Action”, International Journal of Modern Physics D 4 (1995).
