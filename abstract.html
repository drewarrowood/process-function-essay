Every process function lifts to a unitary process

Abstract

The grandfather paradox is an intervention with no fixed point. The bootstrap is an intervention with more than one. A process function is a map from outputs to inputs that is admitted only when every local intervention completes exactly once [16]. Adding a source and a sink turns any map, process function or not, into a permutation:

U(o, e) = (w(o) xor e, o), with inverse U^{-1}(i, s) = (s, w(s) xor i). Both compositions are the identity because xor with a fixed bit is an involution. That is the open case. It does not use the process-function hypothesis, and the identity loop has it.

Closing the output onto the input is a different step. Against local identities, the closed gram diagonal counts fixed points. For the identity loop that number is 2^n, which is 8 on three parties, not 1. If the map is a process function, two distinct strings cannot both be fixed by one local intervention, so some party disagrees in the string and agrees in w [16]. That party is a zero factor in the off-diagonal gram entry, at whichever index the witness sits. The source-to-sink operator is then unitary. Unique completion and the closed unitarity are the same fact read twice.

The argument does not construct a solution of Einstein's equation for the Lugano function. The named metrics are citations [6][7][9]. The 1995 action paper is Carlini, Frolov, Mensky, Novikov, and H. H. Soleng [22], not Solodukhin.

How the citations sit in the text

The lexical ban is Dummett [1][2]. Bilking is Black [3], pressed again by Horwich [21]. The two cans are Lewis [4]. The Stanford survey is Faye [5]. Gödel's metric is [6]. The wormhole time machine is Morris, Thorne, and Yurtsever [7]. Novikov's principle is Friedman and coauthors [8]. The billiard, with one continuation, two, or none, is Echeverria, Klinkhammer, and Thorne [9]. A selection rule is not a dynamics: Earman [10]. Self-consistency from the action is Carlini, Frolov, Mensky, Novikov, and Soleng [22].

Deutsch's fixed point is [11]. The computational power is Aaronson and Watrous [12]. The preparation objection is Bennett, Leung, Smith, and Smolin [13]. Post-selection is Lloyd, Maccone, and collaborators [14]. A Deutsch condition without a curve is Tolksdorf and Verch [20]. The process matrix is Oreshkov, Costa, and Brukner [15]. The process function and the reversible extension are Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf, and Zych [16]. The unitary extension of Lugano is Araújo, Feix, Navascués, and Brukner [17]. Routed quantum circuits, which do not reconstruct Lugano and do not include Ormrod, are Vanrietvelde, Kristjánsson, and Barrett [18]. The conjecture under exclusivity and unambiguity is Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal [19].
