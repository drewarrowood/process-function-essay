Every process function lifts to a unitary process

Draft. Not submitted. References are numbered in order of first appearance.

Abstract

A time-travel story can fail in two simple ways. The traveller tries to prevent the trip, and no version of the story fits. Or the story fits in more than one way, and nothing picks which one. Philosophers have usually answered by saying that the bad stories do not happen, or that can means something different once the whole past is in view. This essay uses a sharper rule. A process is allowed only when every choice the people in it might make finishes the story exactly once [1]. No finish is the grandfather case. Two finishes are the note that writes itself.

There is a reversible way to write any such process down. Add a source, an extra input nobody chose, and a sink, a copy of what was sent. The map that hides the process under the source and stores the output in the sink can be run forwards and backwards, for every process, even the inconsistent ones. That is the open case. It does not yet feed the output back in as the next input. Doing that is the closed case, and it is not well behaved for every process. The process that just copies its output to its input has eight consistent readings on three people, where a good map would have one.

What the unique-finish rule adds is the cancellation. If two possible outputs differ, some participant wrote different things and received the same reply. At that desk the two contributions cancel. So the closed map is reversible precisely when every choice already finished once. The essay does not build a spacetime for the example, and it does not say whether the traveller was free.

1. Two stories, before the definitions

The telegraph office closed at six. Mara had the form that would have kept the man off the train, and no window that would take it. She wrote the opposite of whatever had arrived, and no page could be both the thing she received and the opposite of the thing she would send. That is an afternoon with no finish. The other story is the note in the coat. A blank page and a page of ink both complete. That is an afternoon with two finishes.

Neither story is answered by saying the bad afternoon is rare. The first has no fixed point. The second has two. A process function is the class of maps that have neither failure [1].

2. What the literature did instead

Dummett treated the ban as a rule about English [2][3]. Black asked what happens to the person who tries to stop the later event [4]. Horwich pressed the same demand [5]. Lewis distinguished two meanings of can [6]. Faye still treats the paradoxes as unresolved [7]. Goedel has a closed curve through every event [8]. Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever turn a wormhole into a time machine if the averaged null energy condition fails [9]. Friedman and coauthors state Novikov's principle [10]. The billiard has one continuation, two, or none [11]. Carlini, Frolov, Mensky, Novikov and Soleng derived a version from the action [12]. Earman: a selection rule is not a dynamics [13]. No metric for the three-party example is constructed.

3. Three quantum replies

Deutsch closes a state [14]. Aaronson and Watrous put the power at PSPACE [15]. Bennett, Leung, Smith and Smolin object to the preparation [16]. Tolksdorf and Verch mimic the condition with no curve [17]. Lloyd and Maccone post-select [18]. Oreshkov, Costa and Brukner write a process matrix [19].

4. The rule, stated on the map

A process function is a map w such that for every local intervention f there is exactly one string o with o = f(w(o)). The identity loop and the swap fail. Lugano does not. Araujo, Feix, Navascues and Brukner extend that one function [20]. Routed quantum circuits are Vanrietvelde, Kristjansson and Barrett [21]. Ormrod is not an author of that paper.

5. The open case

U(o, e) = (w(o) xor e, o), and the inverse is (s, w(s) xor i). Both compositions are the identity because xor is an involution. This holds for every w [1]. Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal give two sufficient conditions that are not used here [22].

6. The closed case

Against local identities the identity loop has gram diagonal 2^n. On three parties that is 8, not 1.

7. Why the unique finish is the cancellation

If two outputs differ, some party disagrees in the string and agrees in w. That party is a zero factor, at any index. The closed map is reversible precisely when every choice already finished once.

8. What is not claimed

No Einstein metric for Lugano. The closed contraction is not an isometry for every w. The open bijection is.
