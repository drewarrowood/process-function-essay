Three counts, side by side

Empty, two, and one. The closed window is empty: she writes the opposite of whatever arrived, and no page fits. The note in the coat is two: a blank page and a page of ink both complete the errand. A process is one. The open shuffle inverts all three, because XOR twice is the bit. The closed diagonal distinguishes them. Empty contributes 0. Two would contribute 2 if both were admitted. One contributes 1. The copy rule contributes 8, which is two to the number of desks, the extreme of too many.

Closed diagonal: 0, or 2, or 1, or 8. Only 1 is a process, and only against every intervention.

A referee, in order

The open inverse is not new. It is the source and the sink already in the 2019 paper, written as XOR. The eight-count is the obstruction, written so it can be checked by listing 000 through 111. The witness is unique finish, rearranged: if two strings differ, some party sent different bits and was shown the same bit. Orthonormality does not follow from the process condition. It is an extra premise. Drop the rows and the zero is not forced.

A larger alphabet, as a shape only

A bit is enough for the claims that are proved. A larger alphabet would replace XOR by a group operation with inverses, and the witness would still be a zero at a party where two strings differ and the process agrees. That is the shape of the generalization. It is not a theorem of the file. The stories match the counts. A line element is still not among the premises.
