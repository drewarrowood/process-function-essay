Glossary, the billiard, the stops

Words

A string is what was sent, one bit per desk. A process returns what is shown. An intervention is the local rule at each desk. A finish is a string that agrees with the letters the desks would write after seeing what came back. A process function has exactly one finish for every intervention. The open shuffle adds a spare page and a copy, and can be undone for every rule. The closed desk feeds the letter back in. The diagonal counts strings that equal their image. The witness is a desk where two strings differ and the process returns the same bit.

The billiard, counted the same way

Echeverria, Klinkhammer and Thorne put a hard-sphere ball on a wormhole background. Some initial data have one continuation, some have two, some have none. Those are the same three counts, for trajectories instead of strings. The likeness is the count. The sphere is not a bit, and the wormhole metric is not the process. Novikov keeps data with at least one continuation. A process function also refuses two. That is the difference. Measure zero is a selection. Exactly one is a condition on the map.

Where each literature stops

Prior stops at a line of tense. Pnueli stops at a run of a program. Emerson and Clarke stop at a path quantifier. Lewis stops at two meanings of can. Novikov stops at a measure. Earman stops at the objection that a selection rule is not a dynamics. Ori stops at a dust shell and closed null geodesics. Deutsch stops at a mixed fixed point of a state. Baumeler, Costa, Ralph, Wolf and Zych stop at the open shuffle of a process function. This essay stops at the preimage, the open inverse, the eight-count, and the witness under orthonormal rows. It does not construct a metric.

Checks by hand

Invert the spare bit as in the previous page. List 000 through 111 for the copy rule against identity. Look for a desk where two strings differ and the process returns the same bit. None of that needs a manifold.

The embedding that exists is the discrete bijection. It is not a line element.
