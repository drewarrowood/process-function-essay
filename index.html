Every process function lifts to a unitary process

Draft essay. The open extension is a bijection for every w. The closed contraction is not an isometry for every w.

Open bijection

\(U(o,e)=(w(o)\oplus e,\, o)\) and \(U^{-1}(i,s)=(s,\, w(s)\oplus i)\). Both compositions are the identity by \(a\oplus(a\oplus b)=b\). No process-function hypothesis.

Closed counterexample

Against local identities, the identity loop has \((J^\dagger J)_{0,0}=2^n\). For three parties that is 8, not 1.
