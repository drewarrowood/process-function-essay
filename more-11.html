Two parties

Two bits give four strings: 00, 01, 10, 11. The copy rule agrees with the rule that writes what it is shown on all four. The closed diagonal is 4, which is 2 to the 2. The open inverse still returns the spare page. A swap of the two parties can be given an intervention with no finish. The swap is not a process. It has the open inverse anyway. Four is not one. That is the two-party form of the boundary.

Two parties, copy rule, identity intervention: 00, 01, 10, 11 all finish. Diagonal 4.
