Every process function lifts to a unitary process

Draft. Not submitted. References numbered in order of first appearance.

What was proved, in ordinary words.

Two results are new, and they are not the same result.

The first is bookkeeping. Add a spare page that nobody chose, and a copy of what was sent. The shuffle can be run forwards and backwards for every rule, including rules that do not finish. The spare page comes back because adding a bit twice cancels. Nothing has been fed back into the desk.

The second is the closed desk. Feeding the letter back in is not reversible for every rule. The copy rule has eight consistent afternoons on three desks, not one. Unique finish cancels the extra afternoon: if two afternoons differ, some desk wrote different letters and was shown the same letter. The closed map is reversible only for rules that already finished once.

A process function does not need a metric. It is a condition on messages. The parties are labels, not points of a manifold. No Einstein metric was constructed, and none is used.

Abstract

A time-travel story can fail in two ways. The traveller tries to prevent the trip, and no version fits. Or the story fits in more than one way, and nothing picks which one. A process is allowed only when every choice finishes the story exactly once [1]. No finish is the grandfather case. Two finishes are the note that writes itself.

Adding a source and a sink makes a reversible record of any rule, even an inconsistent one. That is the open case. Feeding the output back in is the closed case, and the copy rule has eight readings on three people. Unique finish is what cancels the extra reading. A metric is a way of realizing the rule, not part of the rule.

1. Two stories

The telegraph office closed at six. Mara wrote the opposite of whatever had arrived, and no page could be both the thing she received and the opposite of the thing she would send. That is an afternoon with no finish. The note in the coat has two: a blank page and a page of ink both complete.

A process function is a map w from sent bits to received bits such that for every local rule f there is exactly one string o with o = f(w(o)).

2. Why no metric is required

Nothing in that sentence is a distance or a clock reading. The open shuffle U(o, e) = (w(o) xor e, o) is a permutation of bit strings. XOR is addition modulo 2, not a displacement. Both compositions are the identity because a xor (a xor b) = b, for every w. Godel, the wormhole, and Ori's dust shell answer whether Einstein's equation can produce a closed curve. They are not premises of the map.

3. What earlier answers did

Dummett treated the ban as a rule about English [2][3]. Black and Horwich asked what happens to the person who tries to stop the later event [4][5]. Lewis distinguished two meanings of can [6]. Faye still treats the paradoxes as unresolved [7]. Godel has a closed curve through every event [8]. Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever turn a wormhole into a time machine if the averaged null energy condition fails [9]. Friedman and coauthors state Novikov's principle [10]. The billiard has one continuation, two, or none [11]. Carlini, Frolov, Mensky, Novikov and Soleng derived a version from the action [12]. Earman: a selection rule is not a dynamics [13].

4. Quantum replies, and the closed counterexample

Deutsch closes a state [14]. Aaronson and Watrous put the power at PSPACE [15]. Bennett, Leung, Smith and Smolin object to the preparation [16]. Tolksdorf and Verch mimic the condition with no curve [17]. Lloyd and Maccone post-select [18]. Oreshkov, Costa and Brukner write a process matrix [19]. Araujo, Feix, Navascues and Brukner extend the Lugano function [20]. Routed quantum circuits are Vanrietvelde, Kristjansson and Barrett [21]. Ormrod is not an author.

Against local identities the identity loop has gram diagonal 2 to the n. On three parties that is 8, not 1. Daher Ahmed and Kunjwal give two sufficient conditions that are not used here [22].
